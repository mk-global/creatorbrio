# Decision register: content automation platform

**Status:** maintained under [#216](https://github.com/mk-global/creatorbrio/issues/216), [#218](https://github.com/mk-global/creatorbrio/issues/218), [#220](https://github.com/mk-global/creatorbrio/issues/220) and [#248](https://github.com/mk-global/creatorbrio/issues/248).

**Date:** 2026-11-18 (revision 3; revision 2 was 2026-11-17; the founder's acceptance of AD-11, AD-35 and AD-46 and the M1 route were recorded on 2026-11-18 under #220; the founder's M1 scope reduction, FD-K, was recorded on 2026-11-23 under #248).

Statuses:
- **ACCEPTED**: decided by the founder, or already decided in an accepted CreatorBrio document that is cited.
- **PROPOSED**: recommended by the [RFC](ARCHITECTURE-RFC.md); becomes ACCEPTED only when the founder accepts it, recorded here with the date.
- **DEFERRED**: deliberately left to a named later stage or proof.
- **REJECTED**: considered and ruled out, with the reason.
- **SUPERSEDED**: replaced by a later founder decision, which is named; the text is kept as it was decided.

A PROPOSED decision is not an authorization to implement; implementation needs admitted issues.

| AD-38 | Every human-attributed record (approval, revocation, reslot, duplicate override, posting confirmation, review comment, stage change) stores the principal that made it as a stable opaque identifier (`person`, `agent-run` or `service` with an id), never a generic actor such as Production v2's `by: "user"`; an approval also stores the workspace and the permission exercised. | AD-39 | Authorization separates four things: authenticated identity, workspace membership, review permission and approval or publication authority. Permissions are `review` (comment and request changes), `approve` for each scope (`research`, `plan`, `realization`, `publication`), `schedule` (set or move a slot, publish now), `connect` (manage connections and credentials) and `administer` (membership and brands). The local owner holds them all. Agent-run and service principals can never hold `approve`, `schedule`, `connect` or `administer`. Delegation means granting a person a permission, never approving on someone's behalf. An approval is in force only while it is not revoked, its subject is unchanged and its approver still holds the permission it exercised; removing a permission first records attributed revocations of exactly the approvals the person holds in force under it, through the owning module's revocation (the publisher's Revoke for publication approvals), made by the person removing it, and takes effect only once they are all recorded, so the publisher never reads permission state. Grants limited to one brand are a DEFERRED extension point for agencies and multi-brand businesses; because an approval records the permission exercised and a carousel's, production's or package's brand never changes, adding that scope later changes no approval record; research-scope grants would first need dossiers to carry a brand (AD-41). | AD-40 | Social connections: a **connection** is one credential grant with one provider, owned by the workspace, with the scopes the provider granted; a **destination** is one platform identity reached through a connection (an Instagram account, a Page, a channel); a brand has zero or more destinations, and the publisher's **account** is one brand's named set of destinations, so a brand may have several accounts and each package targets exactly one.

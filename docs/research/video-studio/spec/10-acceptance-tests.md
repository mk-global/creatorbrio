# 10 · Acceptance tests

[← Specification index](README.md) · Requirements: [01](01-product-requirements.md#requirements-register) · Programme: [11](11-implementation-program.md)

These tests decide whether Video Studio is complete.
They use **real material**: the founder's and the wellness educator's own footage, scripts and brands.
Synthetic fixtures are used only where a test needs a known ground truth (A/V sync, determinism, licence policy).

Each test lists its setup, steps and pass criteria.
**Measured** criteria are checked by code or a measurement.
**Judged** criteria are decided by the named person against the stated rubric.
The release is complete when every test passes ([11 §5](11-implementation-program.md#5-definition-of-complete)).

## Test material

| ID | Material | Provided by |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Founder: 10–15 minutes of raw phone footage for one reel topic, several takes per point, iPhone HEVC HDR, plus a screen recording | Founder |
| M2 | Wellness educator: 10–15 minutes of raw footage for one reel, calm single takes, some outdoor audio noise | Wellness educator |
| M3 | Founder script: "How an AI agent actually works", about 150 words, plus brand | Founder |
| M4 | Wellness topic: "Three breathing techniques for stress", topic and brief only (research required) | Wellness educator |
| M5 | Two reference reels per creator | Both |
| M6 | Synthetic fixtures: flash-and-beep sync clip, licence-policy asset set (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, CC BY-ND, unknown), deliberately faulty project | Generated by the test suite |

## Workflow A: real talking-head footage

### AT-A1 · Ingest of real multi-take footage
- **Setup:** M1 and M2 imported by path.
- **Pass (measured):** every file registered with SHA-256; originals byte-identical after the whole test suite; proxies play and seek in the Studio; HDR clips are tone-mapped by the gate-G2 method; files from one recording session form one source set.
- **Pass (judged, founder):** proxies' colour and skin tones acceptable next to Photos/QuickTime.

**Requirements verified:** [PRD-05](01-product-requirements.md#prd-05), [MED-01](01-product-requirements.md#med-01), [MED-02](01-product-requirements.md#med-02), [MED-03](01-product-requirements.md#med-03)

### AT-A2 · Transcript, takes and delivery analysis
- **Setup:** M1, M2 after ingest.
- **Pass (measured):** word error rate against a human transcript of 3 minutes of each, with the threshold set at gate G3; take groups found for at least the repeats a human marked; every group has a recommended take with a reason; corrected words persist after re-transcription.
- **Pass (judged, creator):** recommended takes are the ones they would choose in most groups (target agreed at G3).

**Requirements verified:** [MED-03](01-product-requirements.md#med-03), [SPC-01](01-product-requirements.md#spc-01), [SPC-02](01-product-requirements.md#spc-02), [SPC-03](01-product-requirements.md#spc-03), [SPC-04](01-product-requirements.md#spc-04), [SPC-07](01-product-requirements.md#spc-07)

### AT-A3 · Selects and rough cut
- **Pass (measured):** no cut falls inside a word; cut points have crossfades; filler and pause candidates are listed and individually restorable; the rough cut duration is within the brief's target range or the overrun is stated.
- **Pass (judged, creator):** the rough cut is a reasonable starting point (no lost meaning, no obvious stumbles kept).

**Requirements verified:** [SPC-04](01-product-requirements.md#spc-04), [SPC-05](01-product-requirements.md#spc-05), [SPC-06](01-product-requirements.md#spc-06)

### AT-A4 · Storyboard from footage
- **Pass (measured):** at least three hook options from what was said; every scene anchors to spoken lines; frames render for every scene with placeholders where assets are missing; per-scene approve, change request and reject all work; a proposed reorder shows the meaning check.
- **Pass (judged, creator):** the storyboard reflects the brief.

**Requirements verified:** [PLN-01](01-product-requirements.md#pln-01), [PLN-07](01-product-requirements.md#pln-07), [PLN-08](01-product-requirements.md#pln-08), [PLN-09](01-product-requirements.md#pln-09), [STB-01](01-product-requirements.md#stb-01), [STB-02](01-product-requirements.md#stb-02)

### AT-A5 · Founder reel, end to end
- **Steps:** M1 + founder brand + founder style profile → full Workflow A with autopilot off.
- **Must contain:** kinetic captions; punch-ins on emphasis words; one animated architecture diagram; text behind the speaker on one key claim; B-roll from the local library and from Wikimedia Commons; at least one Freesound or Earshot SFX on a graphic; music with ducking; an animated insert scene from a Scene Spec.
- **Pass (measured):** technical QA passes; rights cleared for `commercial` usage intent; credits list every sourced asset; deliverable conforms to the Instagram Reel preset; render manifest complete.
- **Pass (judged, founder):** "I would publish this", or a list of changes that are then completed through targeted revisions within the same production (AT-X1).

**Requirements verified:** [STB-06](01-product-requirements.md#stb-06), [PRD-04](01-product-requirements.md#prd-04), [MED-05](01-product-requirements.md#med-05), [SPC-09](01-product-requirements.md#spc-09), [SRC-03](01-product-requirements.md#src-03), [SRC-06](01-product-requirements.md#src-06), [GFX-01](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-01), [GFX-02](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-02), [GFX-03](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-03), [EDT-01](01-product-requirements.md#edt-01), [EDT-04](01-product-requirements.md#edt-04), [VFX-02](01-product-requirements.md#vfx-02), [VFX-03](01-product-requirements.md#vfx-03), [AUD-01](01-product-requirements.md#aud-01), [AUD-02](01-product-requirements.md#aud-02), [EXP-01](01-product-requirements.md#exp-01)

### AT-A6 · Wellness reel, end to end
- **Steps:** M2 + wellness brand + wellness style profile.
- **Must contain:** calm captions; slow push-ins only; key-point cards; denoised voice; soft music; one on-screen claim linked to a source.
- **Pass (measured):** technical QA passes; claim QA shows every on-screen health claim supported; grade preset applied; no effect outside the style profile's allowed set.
- **Pass (judged, wellness educator):** trustworthy, calm and publishable.

**Requirements verified:** [PLN-04](01-product-requirements.md#pln-04), [GFX-01](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-01), [VFX-04](01-product-requirements.md#vfx-04), [QAS-03](01-product-requirements.md#qas-03)

### AT-A7 · Reframing, masking and background
- **Steps:** a 16:9 segment of M1 reframed to 9:16; background blur and replacement on M2; text behind the speaker on M1.
- **Pass (measured):** the face stays within the framing safe area in ≥ 95% of frames; framing keyframes are editable.
- **Pass (judged, founder):** matte edges acceptable at 1080×1920 (gate G5 criterion).

**Requirements verified:** [SPC-08](01-product-requirements.md#spc-08), [SPC-09](01-product-requirements.md#spc-09), [VFX-01](01-product-requirements.md#vfx-01), [VFX-03](01-product-requirements.md#vfx-03), [PLT-03](01-product-requirements.md#plt-03)

### AT-A8 · Audio finishing on noisy footage
- **Pass (measured):** integrated loudness within ±1 LU of the target; true peak ≤ −1 dBTP; music under speech at least the profile's ducking depth; denoise strength adjustable and reversible.
- **Pass (judged, creator):** voice sounds natural, with no pumping or artefacts.

**Requirements verified:** [VOC-07](01-product-requirements.md#voc-07), [AUD-03](01-product-requirements.md#aud-03), [AUD-04](01-product-requirements.md#aud-04)

## Workflow B: script-generated animated videos

### AT-B1 · Topic to researched script
- **Steps:** M4 (topic only) → research → script; M3 pasted as a finished script.
- **Pass (measured):** the dossier lists sources with retrieval dates and reliability; every factual line of M4's script links to at least one claim; health claims flagged sensitive; an unsupported sensitive claim blocks delivery until confirmed or removed; M3 is accepted unchanged; three hook options offered for each.
- **Pass (judged, wellness educator):** sources are appropriate for health content.

**Requirements verified:** [PLN-01](01-product-requirements.md#pln-01), [PLN-02](01-product-requirements.md#pln-02), [PLN-03](01-product-requirements.md#pln-03), [PLN-04](01-product-requirements.md#pln-04), [PLN-05](01-product-requirements.md#pln-05), [PLN-06](01-product-requirements.md#pln-06), [PLN-07](01-product-requirements.md#pln-07), [QAS-03](01-product-requirements.md#qas-03)

### AT-B2 · Script to storyboard with per-scene approval
- **Pass (measured):** scenes cover every script line; frames render from compiled Scene Specs; the animatic plays with the draft read; rejecting scene 3 and regenerating it leaves every other scene's document and compiled output byte-identical.
- **Pass (judged, creator):** the storyboard is a plan they can approve or redirect scene by scene.

**Requirements verified:** [PLN-05](01-product-requirements.md#pln-05), [PLN-08](01-product-requirements.md#pln-08), [STB-01](01-product-requirements.md#stb-01), [STB-02](01-product-requirements.md#stb-02), [STB-03](01-product-requirements.md#stb-03), [STB-04](01-product-requirements.md#stb-04), [STB-05](01-product-requirements.md#stb-05), [STB-06](01-product-requirements.md#stb-06)

### AT-B3 · Local narration with direction
- **Steps:** narrate M3 and M4 with the brand voices, no network.
- **Pass (measured):** narration generated locally, with no paid API; a rate change, an inserted pause, an emphasis and a lexicon pronunciation each produce the specified audible change in A/B listening and in measured timing; word timings within the G4 tolerance of a forced-alignment reference; editing one line regenerates only that line; a cloned voice cannot be created without a consent record.
- **Pass (judged, creator):** voice quality acceptable for publishing (G4 rubric).

**Requirements verified:** [SPC-02](01-product-requirements.md#spc-02), [VOC-01](01-product-requirements.md#voc-01), [VOC-02](01-product-requirements.md#voc-02), [VOC-03](01-product-requirements.md#voc-03), [VOC-04](01-product-requirements.md#voc-04), [VOC-06](01-product-requirements.md#voc-06), [RGT-04](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-04)

### AT-B4 · Founder stick-figure explainer
- **Steps:** M3 → full Workflow B.
- **Must contain:** stick-figure characters with at least five distinct poses and a walk cycle; a narrator and one character voice; shape-based lip sync; an animated architecture diagram revealed on words; a data chart; kinetic captions; SFX on cues; music with ducking.
- **Pass (measured):** technical QA passes; animation deterministic (AT-X9); captions match narration words exactly; deliverable conforms.
- **Pass (judged, founder):** publishable, and the diagram explains the concept clearly.

**Requirements verified:** [STB-09](01-product-requirements.md#stb-09), [PRD-04](01-product-requirements.md#prd-04), [VOC-05](01-product-requirements.md#voc-05), [GFX-01](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-01), [GFX-02](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-02), [GFX-03](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-03), [GFX-04](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-04), [GFX-07](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-07), [EDT-01](01-product-requirements.md#edt-01), [AUD-02](01-product-requirements.md#aud-02), [AUD-03](01-product-requirements.md#aud-03), [EXP-01](01-product-requirements.md#exp-01)

### AT-B5 · Wellness illustrated explainer
- **Steps:** M4 → full Workflow B.
- **Must contain:** illustrated scenes in the brand illustration style, with a recurring character recognisably consistent across at least four scenes; a calm narrator; an ambient bed; key-point cards; one imported Lottie or SVG asset.
- **Pass (measured):** every generated illustration records a commercially usable model in its generation record; rights cleared for `commercial`.
- **Pass (judged, wellness educator):** consistent, calm, trustworthy and publishable.

### AT-B6 · Sourced media and rights
- **Steps:** source at least one asset each from Wikimedia Commons, Openverse, Freesound and Internet Archive into M4's production; then switch usage intent between `personal` and `commercial`.
- **Pass (measured):** each asset has a complete source and rights record; credits text is correct for each licence; a CC BY-NC sound is allowed with a label in `personal` and blocked in `commercial`; an Internet Archive item with unknown rights requires a recorded decision; candidates show a relevance reason.

**Requirements verified:** [MED-05](01-product-requirements.md#med-05), [SRC-01](01-product-requirements.md#src-01), [SRC-02](01-product-requirements.md#src-02), [SRC-03](01-product-requirements.md#src-03), [SRC-04](01-product-requirements.md#src-04), [SRC-05](01-product-requirements.md#src-05), [SRC-06](01-product-requirements.md#src-06), [RGT-02](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-02), [RGT-03](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-03)

### AT-B7 · Direct use of a specialist tool
- **Steps:** a scene uses Manim through the external-tool adapter; the same concept is also built natively as a diagram.
- **Pass (measured):** the adapter output enters the ledger with an invocation manifest; re-running the manifest reproduces the same asset hash for a deterministic tool; the tool cannot write the production; the native alternative is editable in the inspector.

**Requirements verified:** [AIM-06](01-product-requirements.md#aim-06)

### AT-B8 · Documentary collage sequence
- **Pass (measured):** collage built from sourced public-domain or CC images and cut-outs with recorded rights.
- **Pass (judged, founder):** the documentary-collage look is achieved.

**Requirements verified:** [GFX-06](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-06)

## Cross-cutting

### AT-X1 · Targeted revision
- **Steps:** on a finished AT-A5 and AT-B4 production, comment "make scene 3 calmer" and "shorten the diagram's reveal".
- **Pass (measured):** the agent declares its targets before acting; the change set modifies only those targets (ops outside are rejected); final renders of untouched scenes are frame-identical to before; only changed narration lines are re-synthesised; only affected ranges re-render.

**Requirements verified:** [STB-04](01-product-requirements.md#stb-04), [STB-05](01-product-requirements.md#stb-05), [STB-06](01-product-requirements.md#stb-06), [STB-07](01-product-requirements.md#stb-07), [VOC-04](01-product-requirements.md#voc-04), [REV-01](01-product-requirements.md#rev-01), [REN-03](01-product-requirements.md#ren-03), [AIM-04](01-product-requirements.md#aim-04)

### AT-X2 · Undo, discard and non-destructive editing
- **Pass (measured):** discarding an agent run restores the exact prior revision; undo and redo across 50 mixed operations restore identical documents; a labelled version re-renders frame-identically later; originals unchanged; invalid ops leave files untouched.

**Requirements verified:** [STB-08](01-product-requirements.md#stb-08), [PRD-01](01-product-requirements.md#prd-01), [PRD-02](01-product-requirements.md#prd-02), [PRD-03](01-product-requirements.md#prd-03), [PRD-05](01-product-requirements.md#prd-05), [REV-02](01-product-requirements.md#rev-02), [AIM-04](01-product-requirements.md#aim-04)

### AT-X3 · Manual-only production
- **Steps:** with the `manual` runtime, complete a reel from M2 and a short animated piece from M3 by hand.
- **Pass (measured):** every stage can be completed; no screen requires a model.

**Requirements verified:** [EDT-02](01-product-requirements.md#edt-02), [EDT-03](01-product-requirements.md#edt-03), [EDT-05](01-product-requirements.md#edt-05), [AIM-05](01-product-requirements.md#aim-05)

### AT-X4 · Provider-agnostic agents
- **Steps:** run the story director, scene author, edit agent and visual critic on the same task with the Claude Code CLI runtime, the Anthropic API runtime and an OpenAI-compatible local model.
- **Pass (measured):** each produces schema-valid outputs or a typed refusal naming the missing capability (for example no vision); change sets are validated identically; no role needs provider-specific code outside its runtime.

**Requirements verified:** [AIM-01](01-product-requirements.md#aim-01), [AIM-02](01-product-requirements.md#aim-02), [AIM-03](01-product-requirements.md#aim-03)

### AT-X5 · Local core without paid APIs
- **Steps:** after model download, disconnect the network; run AT-A5 and AT-B4 with local libraries only (connectors disabled), with the Claude Code CLI runtime replaced by an OpenAI-compatible local model for this test.
- **Pass (measured):** both workflows complete; the UI states which optional features are off; no paid API or account is used.

**Requirements verified:** [SRC-07](01-product-requirements.md#src-07), [OSS-03](01-product-requirements.md#oss-03), [PLT-02](01-product-requirements.md#plt-02), [PLT-04](01-product-requirements.md#plt-04)

### AT-X6 · Rights gate and credits
- **Steps:** the M6 licence-policy asset set placed in a `commercial` production.
- **Pass (measured):** the policy table in [07 §4.2](07-data-models-and-interfaces.md#42-rights-policy) is enforced exactly; export is refused while blocked assets remain; credits and AI-generation labels are complete; consent is required for a real person's footage marked as not the user.

**Requirements verified:** [MED-04](01-product-requirements.md#med-04), [RGT-01](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-01), [RGT-02](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-02), [RGT-03](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-03), [RGT-04](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-04), [RGT-05](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-05), [QAS-04](01-product-requirements.md#qas-04), [OSS-02](01-product-requirements.md#oss-02)

### AT-X7 · Production bundle round-trip

**Requirements verified:** [EXP-02](01-product-requirements.md#exp-02)

### AT-X8 · Preview equals export
- **Pass (measured):** frames captured from the preview path and from the final render at 20 sampled times match after resolution normalisation (threshold set at G1); fonts, timing and layout identical.

### AT-X9 · Determinism and reproducibility
- **Pass (measured):** two final renders of the same revision have identical frame hashes; render manifests list all versions, models and inputs; generated assets record seeds and engines.

**Requirements verified:** [RGT-05](01-product-requirements.md#rgt-05), [QAS-04](01-product-requirements.md#qas-04), [REV-02](01-product-requirements.md#rev-02), [REN-02](01-product-requirements.md#ren-02), [EXP-04](01-product-requirements.md#exp-04)

### AT-X10 · Technical QA catches faults
- **Steps:** the M6 faulty project (clipped audio, a black gap, a caption in the platform UI zone, too-fast captions, a missing font, a wrong frame rate).
- **Pass (measured):** each fault produces a timestamped finding; delivery is blocked until each is fixed or waived with a reason; waivers appear in the manifest.

**Requirements verified:** [VFX-05](01-product-requirements.md#vfx-05), [AUD-04](01-product-requirements.md#aud-04), [QAS-01](01-product-requirements.md#qas-01), [QAS-05](01-product-requirements.md#qas-05)

### AT-X11 · Creative QA
- **Steps:** the visual critic reviews the six finished productions from AT-A5, AT-A6, AT-B4 and AT-B5 plus two deliberately weak cuts.
- **Pass (measured):** findings are timestamped and mapped to the rubric; the weak cuts receive more and higher-severity findings than the finished ones.
- **Pass (judged, founder):** findings are useful, not generic (rubric agreed at G8).

**Requirements verified:** [QAS-02](01-product-requirements.md#qas-02)

### AT-X12 · Distinct brand styles
- **Steps:** the same script (M3) produced with the founder profile and with the wellness profile.
- **Pass (measured):** caption preset, pacing (pause length, cut rate), palette, voice and SFX density differ as each profile specifies; a reference-reel analysis of M5 proposes profile changes that the user can accept or reject.
- **Pass (judged, both creators):** each output looks and sounds like their brand.

**Requirements verified:** [GFX-09](01-product-requirements.md#gfx-09), [STY-01](01-product-requirements.md#sty-01), [STY-02](01-product-requirements.md#sty-02), [STY-03](01-product-requirements.md#sty-03), [STY-04](01-product-requirements.md#sty-04)

- **Pass (measured):** OTIO and FCPXML from AT-A5 open in DaVinci Resolve with correct cut points and with graphics as alpha clips.

**Requirements verified:** [EXP-03](01-product-requirements.md#exp-03)

### AT-X14 · Performance on the target Mac
- **Pass (measured):** on the M5 Max, a 60 s reel's final render completes within the target agreed at G1; preview scrubbing stays responsive; a one-scene revision re-renders measurably faster than a full render.

**Requirements verified:** [REN-02](01-product-requirements.md#ren-02), [REN-03](01-product-requirements.md#ren-03), [PLT-01](01-product-requirements.md#plt-01), [PLT-05](01-product-requirements.md#plt-05)
